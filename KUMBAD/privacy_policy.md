# Privacy Policy for Kumbad PDF

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

Kumbad PDF is an offline PDF reader and editing toolkit for Android. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## No data collection

Kumbad PDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app:

- Does not require an internet connection to function and requests no INTERNET permission in its release build.
- Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- Does not have user accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.
- Does not send your documents, files, or any content to any server.

## How your files are processed

All PDF viewing, editing, scanning, OCR, and conversion happens entirely on your device using on-device libraries (PDFium, ML Kit's on-device text recognition and document scanner). Your documents are read from and written back to storage you choose on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere by this app.

## Permissions

Kumbad PDF may request the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose and never to transmit data off your device:

- **Camera** – to scan physical documents into PDF using the on-device document scanner.
- **Storage / media access** – to open, save, and share PDF files you choose.

## Third-party libraries

Kumbad PDF uses open-source and licensed libraries (including PDFium via `pdfrx`, Syncfusion PDF, and Google ML Kit's on-device models) that run locally on your device. These libraries do not transmit your document content to any server as used within this app.

## Children's privacy

Kumbad PDF does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
