Kumbad PDF is a fast, private PDF reader and editing toolkit that runs entirely on your phone. No account, no internet connection, no ads, no tracking – your documents never leave your device.

READER
• Smooth pinch and double-tap zoom, powered by the same PDFium engine used in Chrome
• Full-text search with highlighted matches
• Page thumbnails, bookmarks, go-to-page, scroll thumb
• Text selection, copy, and clickable links
• Night reading mode and immersive full-screen view
• Remembers your last page, favourites, and recent files
• Opens password-protected PDFs
• Share, save, and print directly from the app
• Set as your default PDF app – open files straight from WhatsApp, Files, Gmail, Drive, and more

CREATE & CONVERT
• Scan documents to PDF with automatic edge cropping
• Images to PDF
• Word, TXT, CSV, and Markdown to PDF
• PDF to JPG / PNG

ORGANIZE
• Merge multiple PDFs into one
• Split a PDF into separate files
• Reorder, rotate, delete, and duplicate pages
• Extract specific pages

EDIT & SIGN
• Annotate with pen, highlighter, text, shapes, and arrows
• Add images and your own signature
• Whiteout and true redaction (permanently removes content, not just covers it)
• Add watermarks (text or logo)
• Insert page numbers
• Fill out forms and edit document properties

OPTIMIZE & TEXT TOOLS
• Compress PDFs while keeping text searchable
• OCR – make scanned documents searchable
• Extract text (with OCR support)
• Convert to grayscale
• Flatten and repair damaged PDFs

SECURITY
• Protect PDFs with AES-256 password encryption
• Unlock password-protected PDFs you own

Every tool creates a new file – your original document is never modified.

Kumbad PDF needs no internet permission to function. All processing – rendering, OCR, scanning, and editing – happens locally on your device.
